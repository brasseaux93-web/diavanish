# Grants and pilots

Two tracks, one codebase.

- **Mission track.** The open-source core (AGPL-3.0) is self-hosted by NGOs and funded by grants, with pilots at a Geneva convener and a university centre.
- **Commercial track.** A hosted product for ombuds offices, workplace mediation, labour relations and restorative justice, sold on the promise of no transcript to subpoena.

Civilian peacebuilding receives only about 0.5% of development aid ([UN Peacebuilding financing note](https://www.un.org/peacebuilding/sites/www.un.org.peacebuilding/files/documents/panel_discussion_summary_note_-enhancing_partnerships_for_financing_peace.pdf)). The mission track therefore cannot depend on peace funding alone, and the commercial track carries hosting and maintenance.

## Funders that match the mission track

| Funder | Fit | Size and process |
|---|---|---|
| [OTF Internet Freedom Fund](https://www.opentech.fund/funds/internet-freedom-fund/) | Secure communication for people facing surveillance; open source preferred | $10k–$900k for up to 24 months, ideally $50k–$200k for 6–12 months. Concept note first, rolling. Applicants in OFAC-sanctioned countries are not eligible. |
| [OTF Security Lab](https://www.opentech.fund/labs/security-lab/) | Free security audit for internet-freedom tools | Online application. This is the fastest route to the published audit. |
| [NLnet](https://nlnet.nl/funding.html) | Free and open source internet technology | €5k–€50k, deadlines on the 3rd of every odd month. Can include security and accessibility audits. The NGI Zero Commons call has closed, so apply to NLnet's open call. |

Verify current terms on each page before applying; these change.

## The ask, in one paragraph

> Diavanish is an open-source room for confidential dialogue. Chat is end-to-end encrypted and never stored, so there is no transcript to leak or subpoena. Only outcomes that every party approves are kept, on a tamper-evident ledger. We are looking for one facilitated process of 4–12 participants over 6–8 weeks, run by your mediators with your rules. You stay in control, nothing replaces the human process, and we provide setup, a self-hosted or isolated instance, and a published security audit before the pilot starts.

## Sequence

1. Apply to the OTF Security Lab and an NLnet open call at the same time.
2. Fix audit findings and publish the report.
3. Run the first pilot with one convener, using `PILOT_PLAYBOOK.md`.
4. Publish a short evaluation, then apply to the Internet Freedom Fund with pilot evidence.
