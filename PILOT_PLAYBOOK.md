# Pilot playbook

Mediators see their work as human-centred and worry about confidentiality ([Oxford Academic](https://academic.oup.com/book/63025/chapter/566090911)). A pilot should show that the tool keeps both, not ask people to change how they mediate.

## Principles

- The mediator runs the process. Diavanish only enforces the rules they set: turns, caucuses, what goes on the record.
- Summary drafting runs only when the mediator clicks it. It is a fixed template, not an AI model; it works only from approved ledger entries and cannot see chat, which the server cannot read.
- Nothing is recorded that the parties did not approve.
- Paper fallback: every step can continue offline if anyone loses trust in the tool.

## Before

1. **Pick a process** with 4–12 participants, in which one or two sessions would normally take notes that worry people.
2. **Host it** on the convener's own server or an isolated instance. Leave `APERTURE_DEMO` unset (demos are off by default).
3. **Brief the mediator for 60 minutes.** Cover room settings, anonymity mode, role-bound invites, caucus, safety codes, and seal and destruction.
4. **Hand out invites in person or by phone,** never by the same channel as the meeting link. Each invite carries its signed role.
5. **Agree the safety-code ritual.** At the start of each session, the facilitator reads the code aloud and everyone confirms they see the same one.

## During

- Start each session with the safety code.
- If anyone sees a key error or a changed code, stop and rotate (remove and re-admit the seat), or move to paper.
- Use caucus for private conversations and the ledger only for what all parties agree to keep.

## After

- Seal the room. Share the destruction certificate and the verify link.
- Use a 20-minute debrief: did people speak more freely? Did anything feel unsafe? What slowed the mediator down?
- Measure: time to admit seats, number of key errors, number of approved ledger entries against drafts, and participant confidence before and after (1–5).

## Go or no-go for the next pilot

Continue if the mediator would use Diavanish again unprompted, and no participant reports feeling less safe than with their usual setup.
